Privacy by design

RedactShield processes PDFs, images, extracted text, OCR results, redaction regions, custom sensitive words, and exported audit reports on the user's device. The app does not upload document contents to GlobalCore Labs or third parties.

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or telemetry. Optional Pro licensing and purchases are handled through Microsoft Store.

Support

For product support or privacy questions, email support@globalcorehub.com. Do not include confidential document contents in a support message.
